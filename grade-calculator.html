Grade calculator vs. GPA calculator — which do I need?

These solve different problems. This grade calculator works within a single class: finding your weighted average across assignments and tests, or figuring out what you need on an upcoming final. Our GPA calculator works across an entire semester: converting each class's letter grade to grade points and averaging them by credit hours to get your overall GPA. Use this tool while a class is in progress, and the GPA calculator once you have final grades for all your classes.

How the "final grade needed" formula works

Your final course grade is a weighted blend of your current grade and your final exam score. Rearranging that relationship to solve for the final exam score gives:

needed score = (target grade − current grade × (1 − final weight)) ÷ final weight

If the result comes out above 100%, your target grade isn't reachable given how much the final is worth — you'd need extra credit or a grade change elsewhere. If it comes out negative or very low, you've already secured your target grade regardless of the final.

How the weighted average works

Course grades are rarely a simple average — a final exam usually counts for more than a single quiz. The weighted average multiplies each category's score by its weight, adds those together, and divides by the total weight:

weighted average = Σ(score × weight) ÷ Σ(weight)

Dividing by the total weight (rather than assuming it's always 100) means the calculator still gives a correct result even if your weights don't add up perfectly — useful if your syllabus rounds percentages or you haven't entered every category yet.
