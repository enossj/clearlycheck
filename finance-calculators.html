Finance Calculators

Money questions rarely come with a straight answer, so most people guess. This collection replaces the guessing with numbers. Whether you're figuring out a mortgage payment, working out when you'll finally be debt-free, checking whether a purchase actually fits your income, or seeing how compound interest grows a small monthly deposit into something real, each tool does one job clearly and shows its working. Pay-related tools cover the whole earnings picture — convert a salary to an hourly rate, add up overtime at time-and-a-half, and estimate what's left after income tax. Every calculation happens entirely in your browser, so the figures you type never leave your device. None of these replace a professional for big decisions, but they give you an honest starting point in seconds instead of a spreadsheet you never build.
