How Tall Will I Be?

Wondering how tall you will be as an adult? Adult height is influenced strongly by genetics, but growth also depends on a child's growth pattern, puberty timing, nutrition, health, and other factors. A parents-only height calculator gives a useful genetic target, but it cannot account for every factor that affects an individual child's growth.

How Tall Will My Child Be?

This parents' height calculator uses the measured heights of the biological mother and father as an important starting point for estimating adult height. The mid-parental height method calculates a sex-adjusted target height. The American Academy of Pediatrics describes this as a rough estimate with a margin of error of about 4 inches in either direction, so it should be treated as a guide rather than a promise.

What Determines Height?

Genetics & Parents' Height

Human height is a polygenic trait, meaning many genetic variants contribute to adult stature. Parents' heights therefore provide useful information about a child's likely height, although the relationship is not exact and children of the same parents can still differ in adult height.

Nutrition

Adequate nutrition supports normal growth and bone development. Protein, energy, calcium, vitamin D, and other nutrients are important, while significant or prolonged undernutrition or illness can interfere with normal growth. Nutrition does not let a healthy child reliably exceed their genetic growth potential.

Sleep & Physical Activity

Sleep and physical activity are part of healthy childhood development. Good sleep supports normal hormone regulation and recovery, while regular physical activity supports bone and muscle health. These habits help support normal growth but are not a way to override genetic height potential.

Puberty & Growth Plates

Long bones grow at areas of cartilage called epiphyseal, or growth, plates. Puberty changes growth rate and eventually leads the growth plates to close. After growth plates have fused, the long bones no longer lengthen naturally.

How Is Adult Height Predicted?

Mid-Parental Height Formula

This is the method used by this calculator. The parents' heights are averaged, then 13 cm is added to the average for a boy or 13 cm is subtracted for a girl before dividing by two. Equivalently: Boy = (mother + father + 13 cm) ÷ 2; Girl = (mother + father − 13 cm) ÷ 2.

Khamis-Roche Method

The Khamis-Roche method is a statistical model that uses a child's age, current height, current weight, and mid-parental height. The original model was developed from longitudinal data on healthy white American children and is designed to predict adult stature without a skeletal-age X-ray. Because the model depends on a child's current measurements, it is a separate option from this parents-only calculator.

Greulich-Pyle Method & Bone Age

Bone-age methods use an X-ray of the hand and wrist to estimate skeletal maturity. Greulich-Pyle is one commonly used atlas-based approach. A clinician can combine bone age with current height and growth history when a more individualized assessment of remaining growth is needed.

Growth Charts and Height Percentiles

Pediatric growth charts compare height-for-age with a reference population. A 50th percentile means the measurement is near the middle of the reference distribution; a 75th percentile means the child is taller than about 75% of the reference population of the same age and sex. Growth charts are most useful for following a child's pattern over time rather than treating one percentile as a diagnosis. The CDC provides stature-for-age references for children and adolescents ages 2 to 20 in the United States.

How to Get Taller?

Can You Still Grow Taller?

If you are still growing, the goal is to support normal growth rather than chase a guaranteed height increase. Adequate nutrition, sufficient sleep, physical activity, and appropriate medical care can help you reach your individual growth potential. If growth seems unusually slow or fast, a pediatrician can review the growth pattern and decide whether further evaluation is needed.

Can Adults Grow Taller?

Once growth plates have fused, the long bones cannot naturally lengthen. The timing varies from person to person and is linked to puberty, so there is no single age that applies to everyone. Adults can improve posture and spinal alignment, which may change how tall they appear or how they measure during the day, but this is not the same as increasing bone length.
